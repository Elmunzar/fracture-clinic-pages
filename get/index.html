Once it has downloaded

  1. Open the disk image and drag Praxia Clinic Manager into Applications.
  2. Open it. It is signed and checked by Apple, so it starts without any warning.
  3. Sign in with your NHS email address. An administrator in your department has to approve the account before it opens — downloading the app is not the same as being let in.

What it holds

Everything stays on your Mac, encrypted. The app makes no network connections at all. There is nothing to switch off and nowhere for the data to go.
