Privacy policy

Praxia Clinic Manager · last updated 15 September 2026

In short: Praxia Clinic Manager does not collect, transmit or share any data. Everything you enter stays on your Mac.

Who we are

Praxia Clinic Manager is published by EB Apps Development. Contact: ebappsdevelopment@gmail.com.

Data you enter

Referrals, clinics, cancellations and outcomes you enter or import are saved in the app's sandboxed container on your Mac. By default the app records hospital numbers rather than names; names, NHS numbers and dates of birth are only shown and stored if your organisation switches that on in Settings.

Your organisation remains the data controller for any patient information entered. Use Praxia Clinic Manager in line with your organisation's information governance policies, including any data protection impact assessment they require.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no network access, contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and sends no information to EB Apps Development or anyone else. We cannot see your data.

Files

Praxia Clinic Manager only reads files you choose to import and only writes files you choose to export (CSV and PDF). Those files are under your control.

Deleting data

Use Settings → Data → Erase all patients and clinics, or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its container and the data in it.

Children

Praxia Clinic Manager is a professional tool for healthcare staff and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version adds sharing across several Macs through a server run by your organisation, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
