What Pinders does

Pinders routes emergency department and virtual fracture clinic referrals through consultant triage into subspecialty clinics, flags clinics that are full or expected to overflow this week and the next two weeks, recommends extra clinics, records appointment outcomes, and reports on capacity need, cancellations, inter-clinic referrals and routing.

Requirements

macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your Mac, inside the app's private container. Pinders has no network access. See the privacy policy.
How do I bring in referrals or clinics?
Click Add data and choose one patient, one clinic, or an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file. Older .xls files need saving as .xlsx first.
How do I record what happened at an appointment?
Open Clinics, select the clinic, and click Outcome next to the patient.
How do I clear the example data?
Settings → Data → Erase all patients and clinics. Settings, subspecialties and the timetable are kept.
Does Pinders make clinical decisions?
No. Subspecialty, review window, triage and discharge are always set by clinicians. Pinders plans capacity and suggests slots inside the window a clinician chose.
